Launching Fuerza Talent: AI-Ready Experts on Demand

January 19, 2026

The hardest part of building AI-powered products isn't the technology — it's finding senior people who understand both the technology and the product craft needed to ship it. Today we're making that easier with the launch of Fuerza Talent, the productized version of the talent network behind Double Nines.

The Talent Gap in AI Product Development

Every company we work with faces the same challenge: they need to build AI-enabled products, but their existing teams weren't hired for this era. Traditional staffing agencies send resumes of people who've never worked with LLMs, embeddings, or vector databases. Internal recruiting cycles take months. And the few people with real AI product experience command salaries that blow up project budgets.

Meanwhile, there's an incredible pool of senior talent out there — designers who've built AI interfaces, engineers who've deployed production ML systems, strategists who've taken AI products to market — but they're scattered across the Americas and hard to find through traditional channels.

What Fuerza Talent Offers

Fuerza Talent is a curated network of 900+ senior, AI-ready experts — designers, engineers, product managers, and strategists, nearshore across the Americas, vetted for their ability to ship AI-powered products at enterprise scale.

The numbers speak for themselves:

  • 900+ senior experts across design, engineering, product, data, and go-to-market
  • 48-hour average time to hire — not weeks or months
  • 94% retention rate — experts who stay and deliver

How It's Different

This isn't a job board, and it isn't a stack of junior resumes. Every expert in the Fuerza Talent network has been evaluated against a framework refined over 50+ enterprise AI product engagements. We assess not just technical skills but the ability to work within enterprise constraints — security requirements, compliance standards, stakeholder management, and cross-functional collaboration.

When you need a senior React engineer who's built production RAG systems, or a product designer who understands LLM prompt design patterns, or a data scientist who can talk to executives about ROI — Fuerza Talent matches you in hours, not weeks. Engage them on contract, contract-to-hire, full-time, or as a whole augmented team.

Built from Our Own Experience

Fuerza Talent grew out of necessity. As Double Nines placed senior teams into client after client, we built and refined a talent network to draw from. We realized the same network that delivered for companies like Ryder, Bacardi, and Coop could help other enterprises too.

Today, we're opening that network up. Explore Fuerza Talent and see what senior, AI-ready experts can do for your product team.
