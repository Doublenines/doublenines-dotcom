Introducing Fuerza Chat: Every Perspective, One Conversation

March 20, 2026

Today we're launching Fuerza Chat in beta — and it represents a fundamentally different approach to how teams interact with AI.

The AI chat landscape is crowded. ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and dozens of others all offer impressive capabilities. But every model has blind spots. Every chatbot brings a single perspective. And when the stakes are high — product decisions, architecture choices, strategic pivots — a single perspective isn't enough.

The Problem with Single-Model Chat

We've watched teams toggle between three or four AI tools, copying and pasting the same prompt into each one, then manually comparing responses. It's tedious, error-prone, and wastes the very time these tools are supposed to save.

Worse, most teams default to whichever chatbot they tried first. They never discover that Claude might give a more nuanced design critique, or that GPT-4 might catch an edge case in their API schema that the others miss.

Three Layers of Intelligence

Fuerza Chat solves this by bringing three types of intelligence into a single conversation:

  • Your favorite chatbots — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and others respond to your query simultaneously. Compare their answers side by side without switching tabs.
  • Fuerza AI Agents — Our specialized agents, trained on real product development processes from 50+ enterprise engagements, add domain-specific expertise that generic models can't match.
  • Human experts — When a question needs human judgment, senior designers, engineers, and strategists from our Fuerza Talent network weigh in on demand.

How It Works

The experience is simple. You ask a question or share your work — code, designs, strategy docs, whatever. Fuerza Chat intelligently routes your query to the right combination of responders and brings all their responses into a single, coherent thread.

You see where the models agree, where they diverge, and where a human expert adds context that no AI could. Then you decide — with confidence that you've heard from the best minds available.

Built on What We've Learned

Fuerza Chat isn't a standalone experiment. It's the natural evolution of two things we've been building as a senior AI talent firm:

  • Fuerza Agents — our AI agents trained on real product development workflows, now in beta
  • Fuerza Talent — our network of 900+ senior, AI-ready experts who've shipped products for companies like Ryder, Bacardi, and Coop

Fuerza Chat is where these converge — giving teams a single interface to access AI intelligence and the human expertise of the people we place, simultaneously.
