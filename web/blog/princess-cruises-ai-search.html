Supporting Princess Cruises' AI-Powered Search Experience

October 27, 2025

Princess Cruises has built one of the most sophisticated digital experiences in the travel industry. Their platform helps millions of travelers discover and book cruises across thousands of itineraries — and they're constantly pushing the bar higher with new AI-powered features. We're proud to support that work by embedding senior teammates directly into their product and engineering teams.

A Great Product That Keeps Getting Better

Princess Cruises' digital platform is genuinely impressive. The search experience already handles enormous complexity — thousands of sailings with attributes spanning itinerary, ship class, ports of call, onboard amenities, pricing tiers, and seasonal availability. Their team has been evolving this platform for years, and the depth of domain knowledge they've built is something you can't replicate overnight.

What Princess Cruises needed wasn't someone to build their product for them. They needed senior teammates who could embed with their existing team, ramp up quickly, and contribute to the development of new features — especially as they expanded into AI-powered search capabilities using the latest tools and models.

Embedding Senior Talent Into the Team

Our role is straightforward: we provide experienced engineers and designers who join the Princess Cruises team and contribute as full members. They attend standups, participate in sprint planning, review code, and ship features — just like any senior hire, but available faster and with deep experience working on complex enterprise products.

For this engagement, we embedded:

  • Senior Next.js engineers — Experienced with the framework's server-side rendering, API routes, server components, and edge functions. They hit the ground running within Princess Cruises' existing Next.js codebase and started contributing to new AI-powered search features immediately.
  • Senior interface designers — Specialists in search UX and information architecture who work alongside the Princess Cruises design team to craft interfaces that make AI-powered capabilities feel natural and intuitive for travelers.
  • AI-experienced engineers — Teammates who understand the latest AI tools — GPT-4, Claude, embeddings, vector search — and can help the team evaluate, integrate, and optimize these technologies within their existing architecture.

Contributing to AI-Powered Features

The Princess Cruises team has been developing AI-powered search features that let travelers describe what they're looking for in plain language — things like "romantic Mediterranean getaway" or "family cruise with kids activities under $3,000" — instead of navigating rigid filter menus. Our embedded teammates have contributed to this work across the stack.

On the engineering side, our Next.js engineers have helped develop features within the team's existing architecture, working with server components for streaming search results and API routes that connect to AI services. On the design side, our interface designers have contributed to making AI-generated results feel seamless — ensuring that the search experience feels intelligent without being overwhelming.

Performance Management and Optimization

One area where our senior engineers have added particular value is performance management. AI-powered features introduce new performance challenges — LLM inference latency, vector database query optimization, and streaming response orchestration all require careful attention. Our teammates have helped the Princess Cruises team implement monitoring, caching strategies, and graceful degradation patterns that keep the user experience fast and reliable at scale.

Why This Model Works

We've learned that the best enterprise product teams don't need agencies to build things for them. They need great teammates who can augment their capacity, bring fresh perspective, and contribute senior-level skills where they're needed most.

The Princess Cruises engagement is a great example of this model in action:

  1. Fast ramp-up. Our teammates come from enterprise product environments and know how to onboard quickly into complex codebases and team workflows.
  2. Senior from day one. We don't embed juniors who need training. Every teammate we provide is senior enough to contribute meaningfully from their first sprint.
  3. Domain flexibility. As the team's priorities shift — from search features to booking flows to performance optimization — our teammates adapt because they're generalists with deep expertise, not narrow specialists.
  4. Team-first mindset. Our people succeed when the team succeeds. They're not here to run a parallel workstream — they're here to make the existing team stronger.

This is the model we've formalized through Fuerza Talent — a curated network of 900+ senior engineers, designers, and strategists who are ready to embed with enterprise product teams and start contributing immediately.
