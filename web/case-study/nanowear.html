How Nanowear AI Scaled with Fuerza

A business solution delivered by the senior team we placed — Nanowear builds cloth-based nanotechnology and AI for remote cardiac diagnostics. We augmented their engineering organization through the SimpleSense MVP and beyond, scaling the team behind their FDA-cleared remote monitoring platform.

The business solution

SimpleSense is Nanowear's cloth-based wearable that captures ECG, heart sounds, respiration and more for remote cardiac and pulmonary monitoring. As Nanowear scaled after its Series B raise, the senior talent we placed embedded across platform engineering, machine learning, and web — accelerating the path from MVP to launch.

  • Senior platform engineering staff augmentation through MVP
  • Scaled the machine learning engineering team 4x post Series B raise
  • PM, QA, and DevOps management for the web teams
  • Supported integration with launch partners
