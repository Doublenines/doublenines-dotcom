# FamilySearch Guidelines Reference

This document summarizes FamilySearch's key terms, data policies, and compliance requirements. It is a reference summary for development purposes — not legal advice. Always consult the official documents linked below for authoritative information.

---

## 1. Terms of Use

FamilySearch (operated by The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints) provides free genealogical services subject to the following key terms:

- **Personal, non-commercial use only.** You may access FamilySearch content solely for your own personal family history research.
- **No commercial use.** You may not use FamilySearch data, services, or content for commercial purposes, including reselling, republishing, or redistributing data.
- **No reposting or scraping.** You may not copy, reproduce, or redistribute FamilySearch content on other websites or services without explicit permission.
- **No automated access.** Automated crawling, scraping, or data harvesting is prohibited unless authorized via the official FamilySearch API program.
- **Account requirements.** Users must create a free account and agree to the Terms of Use. Accounts are for individual use; sharing credentials is not permitted.
- **Intellectual property.** FamilySearch retains ownership of its platform, software, and curated content. User-contributed content is governed by the Content Submission Agreement (see below).

## 2. Content Submission Agreement

When users contribute content to FamilySearch (e.g., photos, stories, documents, family tree data):

- **Perpetual, worldwide license.** By submitting content, you grant FamilySearch a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free license to use, reproduce, modify, distribute, and display the content.
- **Sharing is permanent.** Once content is submitted and shared, it may be copied by other users and cannot be fully retracted.
- **User responsibility.** You are solely responsible for ensuring you have the right to submit content. Do not submit copyrighted material you do not own or have permission to share.
- **No compensation.** FamilySearch does not provide compensation for submitted content.
- **Accuracy.** Users are expected to submit accurate information and make good-faith contributions to shared family trees.

## 3. Privacy & Data Terms

FamilySearch distinguishes between living and deceased individuals:

- **Deceased persons.** Information about deceased individuals in the shared FamilySearch Family Tree is publicly visible to all users. This is the core collaborative genealogical dataset.
- **Living persons.** Information about living individuals is private by default and visible only to the account holder who entered the data. Living person data is stored in the user's private "My Tree" space and is not shared publicly.
- **Photos and memories.** Photos, stories, documents, and other memories attached to persons in the shared Family Tree are visible to other FamilySearch users by default. Users should be aware that attaching memories to deceased persons makes them broadly accessible.
- **Private spaces.** Users have private areas for storing information about living relatives. This data is not exposed through the public tree or API unless the user explicitly shares it.
- **Account data.** FamilySearch collects standard account information (name, email, etc.) and usage data. Their privacy policy governs how this information is handled.

## 4. Data Processing & Transfer Terms

For organizations and partners working with FamilySearch data at scale:

- **Controller and processor roles.** FamilySearch acts as the data controller for the genealogical data it collects and maintains. Third-party partners or integrators accessing data via APIs typically act as processors and must handle data according to FamilySearch's terms.
- **EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs).** For data transfers involving EU/EEA personal data, FamilySearch's terms incorporate EU SCCs where applicable by region. Partners processing European user data must comply with GDPR requirements.
- **Regional applicability.** Data processing and transfer terms may vary by region. Partners should review the specific terms applicable to their jurisdiction.
- **Data retention.** Genealogical data about deceased persons is retained indefinitely as part of FamilySearch's mission. Living person data is retained as long as the user account is active.
- **API usage.** Developers using the FamilySearch API must comply with the API License Agreement, which includes restrictions on data caching, storage duration, and redistribution.
