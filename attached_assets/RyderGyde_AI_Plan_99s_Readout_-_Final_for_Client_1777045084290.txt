Readiness:  Ready-Only Data promises quick start
Accuracy
Outside of the shop systems, data is believed to be largely accurate, but none of data sources have been QAed for AI uses yet.  
Data Latency
The syncing of data varies between systems and group needs.  Variances arise from human batching of data entry in the shop, timing of batch updates across systems, etc. 
Completeness of Data
Variance in completeness of data is primarily due to telematics level where vehicles can be on different systems and flags can be turned on or off based on contract terms.  Telematics coverage is very high for the largest customers. 
Performance
Some APIs and software components are not designed for or owned by RyderGyde.  Performance levels of tools needed to access data poses obstacles to the timing of their use.   
Ownership
Custom APIs and some 3rd party components are owned by other groups, creating  dependencies for changes in access or timing. Authorization concerns are being addressed.

Future-State Architecture & Delivery Plan
Guiding Principles
Read-only first Initial AI capabilities will be informational and assistive, not action-triggering.
Leverage existing RyderGyde architecture The AI layer will consume existing APIs, caches, and integrations through a controlled orchestration layer (MCP).
Centralized orchestration, not point integrations AI and UI must not directly integrate with backend systems independently.
Security, ABAC, and compliance are non-negotiable AI must inherit RyderGyde’s identity and authorization model.
Observability and QA are first-class concerns AI behavior must be measurable, auditable, and improvable.
28

Future-State Architecture & Delivery Plan
Future-State Architecture OverviewThe future-state architecture introduces five new logical layers, without disrupting existing RyderGyde services.
AI Experience LayerThe customer-facing interface embedded in RyderGyde that enables conversational, read-only access to system insights without exposing backend complexity.
AI GatewayThe control plane that orchestrates AI interactions by managing prompts, retrieval workflows, tool calls, and model execution while enforcing performance, safety, and cost guardrails.
MCP Orchestration Layer (Master Control Program)The centralized domain orchestration layer that aggregates data from RyderGyde and enterprise systems, enforces security and authorization policies, applies caching and performance controls, and exposes AI- and UI-ready APIs.
Retrieval Layer (Vector + Traditional Search)The semantic and keyword search layer that indexes approved operational data and documents to provide accurate, contextual grounding for AI responses.
Central Observability & QA LayerThe cross-cutting monitoring and validation layer that captures end-to-end telemetry, evaluates AI behavior and retrieval quality, supports auditability, and enables continuous improvement through feedback loops.
